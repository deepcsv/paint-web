# Apple V4 process

- 00 Fresh paper and named layers.
- 01 Landmarks, asymmetric silhouette, axis, cross-contours and shadow envelope.
- 02 Even ground/contact shadow streamlines.
- 03 Continuous rough-pencil side-strokes with a raised pressure floor, judged blurred before detail.
- 04 Even traditional-pencil streamlines following the surface field.
- 05 Generate and inspect a value-gated oblique cross-hatch. Its angle gate
  passed, but the layer was rejected and hidden because the pencil preset
  shifted olive against the warm paper.
- 06 Generate a third family for audit, then skip application: the form already
  read clearly and another family would add mesh rather than information.
- 07 Replace the colour-shifted edge pass with neutral graphite; keep only
  selective edges, dimple convergence and the stem cylinder.
- 08 Add restrained paper-coloured pencil lifts.
- 09 Hide construction while preserving its layer.

No image generation or raster import is used. Every visible graphite mark is a
native paint-web stroke with an embedded immutable brush preset. The generator
created 753 auditable native strokes across all candidates; the accepted final
state displays 535 native strokes plus one paper rectangle.
